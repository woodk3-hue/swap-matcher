How long is the BLP?
:
Runs past midnight, public holiday, tax
If it runs past midnight, the next day is
Extra in your pay, after tax —

Rates: BLP/SLP worked 175%. If it runs past midnight into another BLP/SLP it stays 175% for the whole shift. If the next day is a working day, the hours after midnight are paid at that day's rate: Saturday 150%, Sunday 200%, a weekday 100%. Public holiday on a BLP 175%, Labour Day 250%. Hourly rate is your payslip's RATE CPH in cents ÷ 100 (6650.40 = 66.504). Tax is the ATO fortnightly table (scale 2) on top of your usual pay, or about 32% if that's blank. Estimates only; your payslip comes first.
