How matches are worked out

Each line with a date is one week: the date is the Monday it starts, the link number is theirs, and their days off come from the roster (any days named in the post are outlined). If the post says something like “my AMs for your PMs” or “PMs wanted”, the direction is set for you; change it with the buttons if it guessed wrong. Your link for that week is counted on from the link and week you entered, one link a week, wrapping round the roster. A shift starting before 12:00 (or AM1–3, AFA) counts as AM; 12:00 or later (or PM1–3, AFP) counts as PM.

Day by day: your PM and AFP days are better with any AM of theirs (AM3 included); your AM3 days are better with an early AM (AM1, AM2, AFA, or a start before 09:01); your early AMs are worse with their AM3 or PM. Better days have a green box on their row, worse days a red box, and the shifts you want rid of are outlined with a dashed line on yours. Tick PM and/or AM3 under “Shifts I want to swap away”; “Only show weeks where I have these” hides weeks where your link has none. Best match: some days better, none worse, same days off. Good match: your week is PM and you have the same number of days off. Possible: your week is PM but the days off differ. BLP/SLP: each card counts the BLP and SLP days on your link and theirs that week. Balanced means you'd keep the same number of each; a mismatch says how many you'd gain or lose, and days where you're both off but one is BLP and the other SLP are underlined. When the week doesn't balance, it checks the part-week swap rule: if both links have 2 days off (BLP or SLP) between Monday and Saturday you can swap Mon–Sat and keep your own Sunday; if both have 2 days off between Monday and Friday you can swap Mon–Fri and keep your own weekend. Not a match: your week is mostly AM already, or you're on leave or swapped that week. With your backup file loaded, your recorded swaps and leave are taken into account.

Dates with no year are given the year that makes them a Monday, in order down the post. An uploaded roster PDF is read inside this page and remembered in this browser only; nothing is sent anywhere.
